HOW LONG THE BRANDED COMPOSITION IS READABLE
  The name and the tagline fade in over 250 ms and the hand-over to Home starts about 200 ms
  later, so they are seen mid-fade rather than at full strength; most of the start is the
  system splash showing the mark alone. Holding the splash longer would mean delaying the
  start, which this batch forbids, so nothing holds it.

FOUND AND FIXED ON THE DEVICE DURING THE REVIEW
  1. The system splash was plain white. Android 12+ scales a splash icon without a background
     into its own box, and a fixed 84 dp inset left nothing to draw. Now one vector with its
     margin inside it.
  2. The system splash drew white status icons on its white background. The launch theme now
     asks for dark status and navigation bar icons.
  3. The mark was 11 % larger on the system splash (pin 196 against 176 px). The canvas is now
     288 units, the box Android uses (measured about 285 dp), with the mark in the middle 120,
     so both draw it at 120 dp.
  4. The splash cost start-up time: cold 1594 ms against the previous build's 1488, warm 292
     against 213. Its first frame built everything it shows, invisible at that point, and the
     backdrop redrew on every frame of the fade. The first frame now holds only the background
     and the mark; the rest joins on the next frame. Start-up is back to parity, as measured
     above.
